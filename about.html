About

Still learning, still building.

The story so far — where I study, what I'm exploring, and how design and technology fit together in my work.

I'm Gaurab Pantha, a Grade 10 student at Shree Shanti Namuna Secondary School in Tilottama, Nepal.

I spend much of my time exploring the space between design and technology. What started with curiosity gradually grew into an interest in graphic design, coding, electronics, robotics, web projects, and digital media.

Graphic design is one of the areas I enjoy working on most. I create posters, social media graphics, event announcements, promotional content, certificates, and other visual materials.

Alongside design, I'm learning programming and technology through hands-on projects. I enjoy experimenting with web development, Python, JavaScript, browser extensions, electronics, robotics, and new tools.

I'm also actively involved with the Shanti Science ICT & Robotics Club at my school, contributing across marketing, graphic design, media, documentation, and administration.

I'm still learning. I don't want to present myself as someone who knows everything. I enjoy learning by building, experimenting, making mistakes, and improving.
